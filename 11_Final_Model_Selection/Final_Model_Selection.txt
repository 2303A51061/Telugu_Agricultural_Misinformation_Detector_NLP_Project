# Step 11: Final Model Selection

Four classification models were considered for Telugu agricultural misinformation detection: Logistic Regression, Naive Bayes, Linear Support Vector Machine, and MuRIL Transformer.

The models were compared using accuracy, precision, recall, and F1-score. Special attention was given to misinformation recall and F1-score because incorrectly identifying misinformation as genuine information can be a significant concern.

The model with the highest misinformation F1-score was selected as the final model, considering the evaluation results and dataset limitations.

The selected model will be integrated into the prediction system and web application in the subsequent stages.

The final model selection is based on experimental performance on the available dataset and does not establish the model's ability to independently verify real-world agricultural claims.
